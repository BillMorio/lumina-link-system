    Campaign

    Planner

    Swipe for more tabs ›

    Ticked = in the campaign. Unticked creators are ignored from now on: no posts fetched, no orders, their views do not count. This does not cancel them in SideShift. Cancelled = cancelled in SideShift: their posts so far still count, no new posts are expected. Click a creator to see their videos.

    One comment list for this campaign, used on every platform. One comment per line: blank lines and exact duplicates are dropped, at most 300 characters a line. Comments are bought only with a Custom Comments service picked in Edit and "Comments per 1,000 views" above 0. A video never gets the same line twice; the least used lines go first.

    Viral = a post doing far better than the creator's normal. Approved viral posts get a ramp (views bought up to a peak over several days) and the creator's next posts get decay views (a smaller boost that tapers off). Hand-pick adds a post yourself.

    One row per post per day: the views the planner buys that day. Base = normal boost, Viral = a viral ramp, Decay = the tapering boost after a viral post.
